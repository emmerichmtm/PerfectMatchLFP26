PerfectMatchLFP26 - Local Friendship Programme 2026 matcher for 64-bit Windows

1. Right-click PerfectMatchLFP26-Windows.zip and choose Extract All.
2. Open the extracted PerfectMatchLFP26 folder.
3. Double-click PerfectMatchLFP26.exe. Your browser opens automatically.
4. Click "Try fictional example", then "Compute iteration 1".
5. Read manual.pdf (also linked at the top of the page) for the full workflow.

For the real round: choose the survey export CSV on the page, check the
must-haves, compute, review, iterate, then save result.csv and report.txt.
Also save settings.csv and must_haves.csv so the run can be repeated later.
Keep the survey export and all saved files together in a dated folder.

No Python installation or internet connection is needed.
"localhost" refers to your own computer. The files stay on it.
Keep the _internal folder beside PerfectMatchLFP26.exe; do not move the EXE alone.
Click "Close app" when finished: closing the tab alone does not stop the app.
If port 8766 is busy, the app opens another free local address.
If Windows or your organisation blocks this unsigned app, ask IT to review it.

templates\settings.csv         default weights and settings
templates\survey_example.csv   fictional survey export (no real people)
